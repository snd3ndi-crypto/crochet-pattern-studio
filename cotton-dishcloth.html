CROCHET PROJECT GUIDE

Everyday Cotton Dishcloth: Crochet Project Guide

A useful small project to practise even tension, clean edges, and gauge.

Materials

  • Cotton yarn suitable for home use.
  • A hook recommended on the yarn label.
  • Scissors and a yarn needle.

Choose your size

Decide on a target width and height. Make a swatch in your chosen stitch and measure how many stitches fit across 10 cm. Use the homepage stitch calculator for a starting estimate, then adjust for your pattern's stitch multiple.

Project workflow

  1. Make a foundation chain to match your target width, accounting for the turning chain in your stitch.
  2. Work each row in the chosen stitch, beginning and ending in the correct stitch.
  3. Count stitches at the end of early rows to keep the cloth rectangular.
  4. Continue until the piece reaches your preferred dimensions.
  5. Fasten off and weave in the yarn end securely.

Care and finishing

Check the yarn label for washing instructions. Cotton can change shape after washing, so wash and dry your swatch first if exact dimensions matter. A border may change the final size.

Reminder: This is a learning guide, not a substitute for a tested pattern with exact stitch counts. Results vary with yarn, hook, and personal tension.
