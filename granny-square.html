Classic Granny Square: Beginner Crochet Guide

A granny square is a versatile building block for blankets, cushion covers, bags, and colourful accessories.

What you'll need

  • Medium-weight yarn in one or more colours.
  • A crochet hook suitable for the yarn label.
  • Scissors, a yarn needle, and optional stitch markers.

Before you begin

Practise chaining until the yarn moves smoothly around your hook. Learn the chain and double crochet stitches first. Crochet terminology differs between US and UK conventions, so check which terms your pattern uses.

Build your square

  1. Start with an adjustable ring or a short chain joined into a ring.
  2. Work groups of stitches into the centre following a consistent granny-square pattern.
  3. Mark each corner space to find it easily on the next round.
  4. Repeat rounds, keeping the sides and corner groups consistent.
  5. Fasten off, weave in ends, and gently block if suitable for your yarn.

Tips for even squares

If your square cups, tension may be too tight or the pattern may need more stitches. If edges wave, check for extra stitches in the corners. Count the groups on each side before beginning a new round.

Make it a bigger project

Join several squares into a blanket, pillow cover, or tote panel. Finish one test square and measure it before making a large batch.

Reminder: This is a learning guide, not a substitute for a tested pattern with exact stitch counts. Results vary with yarn, hook, and personal tension.
